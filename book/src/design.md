# Design — Withheld

**Canon core:** Her Governor game — edging as a control-systems game.

## Mechanism group

- The Governor core (fixed threshold; the drive cuts at the top and latches off — winning is riding the line without tripping it)
- Threshold presets per session contract
- The begging register as an input — a well-formed ask moves the next session's schedule, never the current wall
- Countdown presentation in the Counted Down shape
- Denial bookkeeping — the term is recorded and carried

## What this entry validates

Denial as a feel, not a description, and begging as mechanics rather than flavour text — in Torogao's native language, so the control law ports back without translation.
