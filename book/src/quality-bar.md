# The quality bar — Laplace3D parity (D-196)

Minimum quality is **Laplace3D-class animation, or the equivalent for this
game's medium and animation style** — as good as or better than the Laplace3D
work catalogued on Danbooru, measured against the estate's extracted criteria
(`animation-laplace-parity-20261027/PARITY.md`), not against vibes:

- **60 fps presentation** (1920×1080 class), motion on **every frame** — no
  step quantization.
- **Two-body competence** where the scene is two-body: coupled motion, bodies
  that read as in contact, not two solos pasted together.
- **Humanized timing**: cycle peak-spacing jitter in the 0.43–0.47 s class —
  never metronomic, never frame-identical repeats.
- **Secondary motion** (hair, clothing, soft tissue): phase-locked, lag
  **≤ 17 ms**.
- **Expression readability at presentation size**: at peak, iris fraction
  0.20–0.35 of the eye opening, sclera ≈ ⅔ (the F-05 form); face states must
  survive at the size the player actually sees.
- **Seamless loops**: loop seam ratio ≤ ~1.1 (loop step ÷ median step).
- **Sync**: voice/reaction peaks land ~+12 ms (±25 ms) against the motion
  event clock.
- **Determinism**: fixed-step evaluation; identical re-renders byte-identical
  where the medium allows it.

Anything below this bar is reported as below bar — never settled for, never
renamed. Gaps close curatively, with before/after measurements.
