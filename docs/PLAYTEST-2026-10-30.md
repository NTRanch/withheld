## Method

- `node tools/playtest.mjs`: the shipped wasm core in demo mode rides
  each term to a terminal screen. Pass = victory with zero trips.
- `node tools/settings-check.mjs`: settings bridge round-trip;
  petition bridge round-trip + cap clamp; settings reachable from
  boot, ungated.
- `node tools/perf.mjs`: step budget.
- `cargo test`: 15 tests, validation + adversarial split; clippy
  `-D warnings` and rustfmt clean (pin nightly-2026-09-25, resolved
  clippy 0.1.100).

  MP4s at viewing size and inspected — SHOULD: a centred figure whose
  face zone reads at a glance, the gauge with its gold WALL line and
  rose band, the term as huge numerals, no text collisions. First pass
  found one FAIL (ride line vs controls hint overlap); fixed,
  re-captured, re-inspected PASS.
